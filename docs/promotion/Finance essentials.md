# The accounting equation

**Every asset has a source of funding.**

$$\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity}$$

| Assets | Liabilities | Equity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $50,000 | $20,000 | $30,000 |

> [!tip] Read the relationship
> What the business owns equals what it owes plus the owners' share.

# Profit and cash flow

**Profit and cash tell different stories.**

- **Profit** records income and expenses for a period.
- **Cash flow** tracks money moving in and out.

> [!example] A sale on credit
> Revenue may be recognized today, while the cash arrives next month.

# Working capital

**A quick view of short-term liquidity.**

$$\text{Working capital} = \text{Current assets} - \text{Current liabilities}$$

| Current assets | Current liabilities | Working capital |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $18,000 | $11,000 | $7,000 |

Positive working capital means current assets exceed current liabilities.[^liquidity]
